Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work with my current phone system?

Yes! We work with any phone system - landline, VoIP, mobile. Simply forward calls to our number. No hardware or software installation required.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are live within 24 hours. We handle everything - you just provide your business info, menu/services, and operating hours.

What if a customer wants to speak to a human?

The AI can transfer calls to you or your staff anytime. You set the rules - urgent matters, complaints, or on-request transfers.

Can it handle accents and different languages?

Our AI understands diverse accents perfectly and can operate in multiple languages. We currently support 15+ languages.

Can I customize what the AI says?

Absolutely! You control the greeting, responses, upsells, and personality. Want it casual and friendly? Professional and formal? You choose.

How do I track calls and orders?

Everything is logged in your real-time dashboard - call recordings, transcripts, orders, appointments, and analytics.
